About

Model Home Incorporated

Model Home Inc. is a premier contracting company specializing in high-quality flooring and cabinetry solutions for residential and commercial spaces. Our mission is to transform homes and businesses by providing exceptional craftsmanship and innovative designs that cater to the unique needs of our clients. At Model Home Inc., we offer an extensive selection of flooring options, including hardwood, laminate, tile, and luxury vinyl, ensuring that every project reflects the customer's style and preferences. Our cabinet services range from custom designs to efficient installations, enhancing the functionality and aesthetic appeal of kitchens, bathrooms, and living areas. With 20+ years of experience and a team of experienced professionals, we pride ourselves on our attention to detail, personalized service, and commitment to excellence. Whether working on new constructions or renovations, Model Home Inc. is dedicated to delivering results that exceed expectations.
