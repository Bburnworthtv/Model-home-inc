ALL ABOUT MODEL HOME INC.

Crafting Your Dream Spaces

At Model Home Inc., we are dedicated to transforming houses into homes that reflect the dreams and personalities of those who inhabit them. With a rich heritage in offering top-tier home design solutions, our portfolio spans elegant wooden and waterproof flooring, bespoke kitchen designs ranging from custom to semi-custom and RTA options, luxurious bathroom fittings, and stylish doors and moulding. Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction is the cornerstone of our business. We blend traditional craftsmanship with modern technology to create spaces that are not only beautiful but also functional and durable. Whether you're renovating a single room or designing a new home, Model Home Inc. is your partner in bringing your vision to life, ensuring every detail is perfected to create the backdrop for your life's most cherished moments.
